What is Time Series
Forecasting?

Definition

Time Series is a regression model designed to predict future values
using patterns observed in past data over time. It focuses on data
with a time dimension - every observation depends on when it

occurred.

The key model for:
1.Supply chain: short-term demand forecasting, inventory.
2.Website traffic: predicting daily visitors.

3.Energy usage: forecasting electricity demand.
