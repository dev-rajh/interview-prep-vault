Introduction —

If you've ever wondered how companies like Netflix recommend
movies, how banks track millions of transactions, or how apps manage
your data seamlessly, the answer often lies in SQL.

SQL (Structured Query Language) is the language of data, and
mastering it is one of the fastest ways to unlock careers in data
analysis, backend development, and beyond.

In this roadmap, you'll not only learn the core SQL concepts step-by-
step but also apply them immediately through problems designed to
mimic real-world scenarios.

Think of it as your gym for SQL, where every concept comes with a
workout to strengthen your skills,

By the end, you won't just ‘know SQL’ you'll be able to use SQL to solve
meaningful problems and stand out in interviews or projects,
