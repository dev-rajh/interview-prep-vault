5. EXISTS

Employed when you want to check for the existence of
records in a related table or subquery. Helps when
you do your EDA.

It returns a TRUE if the subquery returns at least one
row,

lt is more performant than using IN or JOIN.

SELECT department_name
FROM departments d
WHERE EXISTS (

SELECT 1

FROM employees e

WHERE e.department_id = d.department_id
)5
