Question #6: Top N Records Per Group
The Question:

"Find the top 3 highest-paid employees in each
department."

What they're testing:
Do you understand partitioning and ranking?

Most people get this wrong |

This is where window functions shine.

Swipe for the answer

The Solution:
WITH ranked_employees AS (
SELECT
department,
employee_name,
ROW_NUMBER() OVER
(PARTITION BY department
ORDER BY salary DESC) as rank
FROM employees
)
SELECT
department,
employee_name,

salary
FROM ranked_employees
WHERE rank <= 3;

Key phrase: "PARTITION BY resets the ranking for each group”
Follow-up they'll ask: "What if you want bottom 32"
Your answer: Change ORDER BY salary DESC to ASC
