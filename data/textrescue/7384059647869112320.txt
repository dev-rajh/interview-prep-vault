Dreaming of Deloitte? Let's talk Data Engineering Interviews!

Many aspirants fear Big4 interviews, but here's the truth: Deloitte focuses on your core problem-solving skills, not just buzzwords.

Some real Data Engineer interview highlights:
- Write a query to fetch the Top 3 salaries (think DENSE_RANK()!)
- Explain the difference between clustered & non-clustered indexes
- How do you optimize a slow SQL query?
- DELETE vs TRUNCATE vs DROP -> know the impact!
- Ensuring data quality in ETL pipelines (nulls, duplicates, schema checks)
- Pandas vs PySpark -> when to use what?
- Snowflake vs BigQuery -> understanding architectures
- Role of Kafka in real-time pipelines

If you're prepping, don't just memorize syntax. Focus on why a particular approach is better in a given scenario (trade-offs matter).
Pro tip: Pair every answer with a real-world example from your projects.
